Search — before you type

Three directions for the empty Search page. Genres and their counts, the DJs, Traumer's gigs and the track names come from your library; the small numbers (new likes, sets owned, "3 sets saved") are examples. Pick one, or mix parts.

A · Jump back in

Recent searches as chips, then rows of what you used last: recently played tracks, your DJs (round photos → DJ page), and recently added. Feels like Spotify's search home. Cheapest to build: everything is already stored (play history, DJ profiles, date added); recent searches are kept on this machine.
