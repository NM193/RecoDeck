When it collapses

By hand: the collapse button, or ⌘\. RecoDeck remembers your choice.
By itself: when the window is narrower than ~1100px it collapses, and expands again when you widen it — unless you collapsed it by hand, which always wins.

What the menu does
• 8 colours in one row; the current one is ringed.
• Custom colour… opens the system colour picker for any colour.
• Reset to default puts back the colour the section started with.
• Works the same in the icons-only sidebar: right-click the icon.
• Folders, Playlists and Spotify headers get the same menu for their icon.

Nothing goes in Settings, so there is no second place to keep in sync.
