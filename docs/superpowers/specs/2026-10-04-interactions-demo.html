Interactions — try them

Everything here works: hover, click, hold, use the keyboard, drag. One tempo everywhere: fast 120ms (hover, press), base 180ms (menus, sections), slow 240ms (panels, pages, toasts). Every button 6px.

1 · Hover, press, focus

Hover: lighter. Hold the click: the button presses in slightly. Tab from the keyboard: a blue ring (keyboard only, not the mouse). "Analyze": a spinner and "Analyzing…" while it works, then a confirmation.

4 · Loading and feedback

Nothing is blank while it reads: grey row shapes shimmer, then the rows fade in (240ms). After every action a short confirmation, bottom-centre above the player, with Undo where it can be put back. An error stays until closed.
