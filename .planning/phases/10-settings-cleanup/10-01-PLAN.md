<objective>
Remove Key Notation and Waveform Style as user-configurable settings; harden Camelot and Traktor RGB as fixed defaults. Reduce the Settings > Appearance theme list to Midnight and Carbon only.

Purpose: Eliminates two settings that served no user-visible purpose — Camelot is the only stored key format in the DB, and Traktor RGB is already hardcoded in WaveformVisualizer. Removing them eliminates dead state, dead IPC calls, and dead prop drilling throughout App.tsx.

Output: Smaller, cleaner settings layer with no openkey branching, no waveformStyle state, and only two theme choices in the UI.
</objective>
