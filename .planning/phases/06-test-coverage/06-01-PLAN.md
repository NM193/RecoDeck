Expect tracks where bpm_ok OR key_ok is true.

8. `test_build_seed_context_no_seed_data_falls_back` -- Call `build_seed_context` with `seed_bpm: None`, `seed_key: None`, direction "maintain". Should fall back to full context (up to 5000 tracks). Verify result is Ok and contains all tracks.
